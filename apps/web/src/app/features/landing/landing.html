How iThemba works

From sign-up to seeing a doctor — in four steps

01
Register

Sign up via the web app or M-Pesa super app. KYC is verified through Vodacom M-Pesa. One-time R67 registration fee.

02
Save

Contribute R500–R10,000 per month via M-Pesa or debit order. Funds sit in your personal Health Savings Account.

03
Book

Search for accredited doctors, dentists, and specialists near you. Book an appointment in seconds.

04
Pay

Receive a 6-digit visit code by SMS. Show it at the clinic. Payment is authorised instantly from your HSA — no paperwork.
