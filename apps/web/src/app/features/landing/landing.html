How iThemba works

From sign-up to seeing a doctor — four steps, no paperwork

01
Register

Sign up, verify your email and phone. A once-off R67 fee opens your Health Savings Account instantly.

02
Save

Top up R500–R10,000 per month via M-Pesa or C-Pay. Your money is held in your personal HSA — for healthcare only.

03
Book

Find accredited doctors, dentists, and specialists near you. Book an appointment online in seconds.

04
Pay

Get a 6-digit visit code by email. Show it at the clinic. Payment debits your HSA instantly — no forms, no waiting.
